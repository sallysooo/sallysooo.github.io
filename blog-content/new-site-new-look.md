I finally got around to rebuilding this site from scratch, and it's live.

The old version ran on Jekyll, using the [al-folio](https://github.com/alshedivat/al-folio) theme. It served me well for a long time, but I'd outgrown what it could easily do. This time I rebuilt everything on React and Vite instead, which gave me a lot more room to shape the site the way I actually wanted it to feel.

A few things changed along the way:

- **Projects, reimagined.** Instead of linking out to a separate page for every project, each card now opens as a quick-view panel right on top of the grid: screenshots, links, and write-ups included, without losing your place in the list.
- **A real Experience timeline.** Research internships, lab work, and club activities (BOAZ, SNSec Lab, SISS) now sit on one continuous timeline, with photos and sub-activities nested where they actually belong instead of scattered across separate pages.
- **This blog.** A space for shorter, less formal posts: conference notes, small experiments, and updates like this one, kept apart from the more polished Projects section.
- **A handful of small details** I probably cared about more than I should have. Consistent light and dark theming. A favicon that's 🍵 again. And, if you poke around the homepage for long enough, something extra hiding in plain sight.

Everything still lives at the same address, [sallysooo.com](https://sallysooo.com). If you notice anything broken, I'd genuinely like to hear about it, so feel free to reach out through any of the links on the home page.

More posts coming soon, hopefully about actual research rather than site plumbing.
